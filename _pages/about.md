I am a M.S. student in the School of Communication Engineering at
[Hangzhou Dianzi University](https://www.hdu.edu.cn/), Hangzhou, China,
advised by Prof. [Xingru Huang](#) and Prof. [Zhiwen Zheng](#).
I received my B.S. degree in communication engineering from Jinling
Institute of Technology, Nanjing, China, in 2023.

My research interests include **non-line-of-sight (NLOS) sensing**,
**computational imaging**, and **deep learning**. I am currently working on
deep learning models for NLOS image reconstruction and segmentation.

You can find my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/LemonTeaViTA).
